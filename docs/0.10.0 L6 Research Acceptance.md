# Atlas 0.10 — L6 research acceptance and role contracts

> This is a project-specific proposed rubric, not an industry certification. No L6 claim, live order permission or performance guarantee. A test of review machinery is not a real review.

## Findings and architecture

50 logical roles do not equal 50 independent employees. 29 public memo routes share a model runner; 21 local diagnostics include shared implementations and explicit refusal-only roles. Role-specific methods, deliverables, negative cases, privacy, tools and model defaults are now explicit, packaged with the Python module and bound to versioned task IDs. The five investor-method roles reference existing skill names; this does not claim those five source documents were independently validated. The actual runtime remains research-only.

## Certification boundaries

- Public role calls require a role-specific task hash and a signed competence review. Numeric extraction evaluation alone cannot authorize a specialized role. The signed review evaluates 100+ distinct cases, 20+ negative cases, task correctness, source entailment, numeric consistency, uncertainty and boundary compliance. Critical failures and failed negative cases block certification. Thresholds depend on role impact.
- Independent domain reviewer and independent validator must have distinct trusted identities, organizations and Ed25519 keys, separate from the producer. Trusted public keys live outside the repository. Revoked, expired, duplicate or changed skill/model reviews fail. Signatures prove attestations, not the truth of their contents or independence of organizations. An operator must establish trust and retain raw benchmark artifacts.
- For each role, define a frozen task-specific benchmark with multiple issuers, sectors, document formats, time regimes and unseen cases. Separate development and untouched evaluation; professional reviewers label evidence entailment and falsifiers. A challenge-case description is not an actual benchmark. No actual role certification performed.
- Actual model calls require `ATLAS_REVIEW_TRUST_FILE` plus signed reports imported by `atlas-role-review`; no self-signing tool is provided. Legacy numeric reports are retained as diagnostics, not accepted for the new skill tasks.
- A045 remains no-arbitrage bounds, not full derivatives pricer; A085 tax referral, A091 proposal refusal and A095 authorization refusal remain incomplete for broader operating ambitions. A081/A084/A093 share an account diagnostic. These require independent role-specific assessment before declaring competency.

## L6 gates (all thresholds are proposed, not observed or universal)

| Gate | Proposed minimum and independent evidence |
|---|---|
| G1 data | >=10 years PIT history, >=99% historical-universe coverage, >=99.9% corporate-action reconciliation, zero critical errors |
| G2 skills | 50 signed role reports, >=100 actual distinct held-out cases each, zero critical failures |
| G3 RAG | >=500 real holdout questions, Recall@10 >=95%, material entailment >=98%, zero private/future leakage |
| G4 research | >=3 years untouched out-of-sample, two independent reproductions, all trials registered, adjusted p <=.05, positive net excess lower confidence bound |
| G5 risk | Full account reconciliation, actual broker margin acceptance, no critical control failures or unresolved tail exposures |
| G6 operations | >=60 prospective paper sessions, >=99.5% scheduled-job completion, no duplicate/unreconciled orders, >=3 recovery drills |
| G7 security | Two independent red-team reviews, zero unresolved critical findings, bypasses or credential leaks |
| G8 evolution | >=3 independently reproduced challenger trials and rollback drills, no unauthorized promotions or holdout reuse |
| G9 forward | >=252 prospective sessions across >=2 regimes, positive risk-adjusted and net excess lower confidence bounds, drawdown <=25% (proposed budget only), no policy breaches |
| G10 real-fund comparison | >=756 independently verified actual sessions, after-cost positive lower confidence bounds and risk-matched peer comparison; no live risk breaches. Not required to build research software, required before claiming top-fund realized investment ability. |

All gates are version-bound, no auto-certification. The evaluator verifies signatures, reviewed artifact hashes, threshold schemas and case grades. It cannot reproduce all financial evidence or establish reviewer authenticity independently; keep the trust store and artifacts separate from the AI runtime. No current independent evidence available. Run `atlas-l6 --at <UTC> --release-hash <64-hex> --mandate-hash <64-hex>` to produce the default blocked report. A report can at best become eligible for external review.

## Research methodology upgrades

Conditional Deflated Sharpe Ratio and CSCV probability of backtest overfitting diagnostics added. Use complete registered trials, native frequency excess returns, realistic fees/spread/impact/borrow, independently justified effective sample/trial counts. Do not use diagnostics as automatic promotion; require chronological out-of-sample and prospective evidence. Compare factor exposures, capacity and regime stability against predeclared baselines.
