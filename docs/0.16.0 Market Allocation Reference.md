# Market allocation reference experiment (0.16)

This is retrospective developer research, not a new holdout, an investable backtest, or L6 certification. The previous 0.15 risk study found no model that met both 95% and 99% conditional coverage tests. No risk policy or trading permissions change.

Four fixed policies, same 2005–2024 market portfolio and research risk-free return: buy and hold, monthly 70/30, daily 200-day trailing-return trend, weekly 10% annualized-volatility target capped at 100% risky weight. Initial history begins 2004-01-02. Signals are lagged through session t-2 and the target is modeled at close t-1, with return realized on t. Research series is not executable and lacks original vintages. Every policy is tested at 10, 25 and 50 bps one-way cost. No tuning or automatic selection. Rebalance fees are solved on post-cost target weights. The nine active cost/strategy comparisons are counted in Bonferroni HAC diagnostics; subperiod results are descriptive only. The buy-and-hold baseline is charged its initial entry fee. No leverage, shorting, live or paper broker orders. Raw archive is not published. The source digest is fixed to the prior verified archive.

Run with an authorized local archive: `python -m atlas_pro.market_strategy --zip PATH --confirm-research-rights`. GitHub workflow runs the same study with a restricted same-repository branch and read-only token. No raw data artifacts. The study does not establish G1, G4, G5, G8, G9 or any other L6 gate.
