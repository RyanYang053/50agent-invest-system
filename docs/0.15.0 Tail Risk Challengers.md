# Thick-tail risk challenger protocol

Objective: address underestimation of 99% daily loss in the prior market risk diagnostic, without changing the investment mandate or trade permissions.

- Source: same official December 2024 Dartmouth Fama/French daily factor archive; personal research rights confirmation required. Do not publish raw licensed data. Market return is Mkt-RF + RF, divided by 100. Validate full XNYS session coverage, archive size and hash.
- Fit period: 2004-01-02 through 2014-12-31. Evaluation: 2015-01-01 through 2024-12-31, trading sessions only. Prior 0.14 already inspected this archive; this is retrospective chronological separation, NOT independent holdout validation.
- Fixed competitors: 252-day historical empirical quantile/ES; EWMA-normal (0.94); EWMA-t with variance-standardized df=5; EWMA-filtered historical simulation using last 750 causal standardized returns; stationary GARCH(1,1)-t with fixed fitted parameters and daily variance updates. GARCH optimizer uses three fixed starts, rejects failure, fits only training data. All competitors use the same evaluation dates.
- Metrics: realized breach rate, Kupiec unconditional coverage, Christoffersen independence and joint coverage, mean pinball loss, descriptive unconditional ES identification moment with Bartlett HAC lag 20. Nonrejection is not proof of calibration. Multiple models/levels are inspected, no inferential claim of superiority. Formal joint VaR/ES tests and independently preregistered prospective replication remain open.
- Limitations: reconstructed market return archive, not historic PIT vintages, no account holdings, leverage, options, liquidity, intraday losses, broker margin or strategy returns. No promotion authority. Report failures; do not choose a winner from this reused sample.
