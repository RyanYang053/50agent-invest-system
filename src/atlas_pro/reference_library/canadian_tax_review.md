# Canadian account and tax research checklist
This is a dated rule-verification procedure, not legal or tax advice. All personal records stay local.
1. Identify tax residence, account type, action date, security, cost basis, currency and ownership. Distinguish TFSA, taxable and other account rules; do not apply a rule from one account to another.
2. Verify current official CRA guidance and effective date. Reconcile contribution room to actual contribution and withdrawal records; do not infer contribution room from market value or investment loss.
3. For taxable accounts request adjusted cost base, acquisition/disposition records, fees, FX convention and relevant related-account activity. Identify possible loss restrictions or withholding for human tax review rather than making unsupported conclusions.
4. Calculate only when the required evidence and applicable rules have been independently reviewed. Record tax assumptions and scenarios separately from investment merit.
5. No automatic tax optimization, account transfers or orders. Escalate cross-border, derivative and ambiguous cases.
Acceptance: test missing records, stale guidance, unsupported loss assumptions and incompatible account types. Knowledge expires when rules change.
Official CRA reference: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/tax-free-savings-account/contributing/how.html .
