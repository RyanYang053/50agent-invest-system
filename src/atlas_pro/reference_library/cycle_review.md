# Credit and valuation cycle review
Purpose: diagnose risk appetite and financing conditions, not forecast a turning point.
1. Freeze the observation time and define the market segment. Retrieve dated short/long yields, credit spreads and relevant equity valuation measures. Treat series without verified release time as unavailable in historical replay.
2. Split observed conditions into financing cost, credit availability, balance-sheet fragility and valuation. Compare rolling percentiles using history strictly before the decision timestamp. Record period and sample count; percentiles are not probabilities.
3. Compare plausible recession, inflation and risk-appetite explanations. Identify which signals disagree and what observable result would overturn the cycle diagnosis.
4. Report downside mechanisms, exposure sensitivity and missing evidence. Do not turn a cycle label into an order, exact market bottom or guaranteed recovery.
Validation: replay a dated episode; recompute percentiles; verify source vintage; check whether later revisions alter the classification; retain the null hypothesis.
Learning: register errors in signal timing or classification after observable outcomes, not from subsequent narratives alone.
References (not ingested facts): FRED/ALFRED https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/alfred.html ; Damodaran https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/tools.html .
