# Inversion, base rates and multi-disciplinary bias checks

Status: **Draft method decomposition — NOT a personality clone or claimed endorsement.**

Objective: use original, attributable public sources to build a repeatable investment checklist. No unauthorized copying of licensed books, no invented quotes or personalized investment-person impersonation.

## Applicability

- all public-equity theses

## Required method checks

1. invert the hypothesis: what causes permanent loss?
2. identify leverage, dilution and adverse selection
3. list incentives and principal-agent risk
4. check survivorship/recency/availability biases
5. test alternative explanations
6. compare base rates and forecast error
7. identify neglected disconfirming evidence
8. estimate opportunity cost
9. design monitorable thesis breakers
10. produce no-trade alternative

## Abstain / escalate when

- insufficient independent evidence to form a specific prediction
