Status: **Draft method decomposition — NOT a personality clone or claimed endorsement.**

## Applicability

- established businesses
- cash-flow-producing listed firms

## Required method checks

1. understand revenue and unit economics
2. historical ROIC versus reinvestment
3. moat evidence and erosion signals
4. management incentives and capital allocation
5. owner-earnings versus accounting profits
6. leverage/refinancing exposure
7. cyclically normalized cash flow
8. conservative intrinsic value range
9. price versus safety margin
10. thesis falsifiers

## Abstain / escalate when

- business economics impossible to understand
- no reliable normalized earnings or cash flow
