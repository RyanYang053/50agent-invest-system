Status: **Draft method decomposition — NOT a personality clone or claimed endorsement.**

## Applicability

- equity and credit linked exposures

## Required method checks

1. identify credit and liquidity regime
2. separate asset quality from price
3. state what consensus expects
4. measure spreads and valuation dispersion where available
5. check funding and positioning vulnerability
6. stress downside scenarios
7. compare probabilities without false precision
8. evaluate forced-seller dynamics
9. identify regime shift triggers
10. document contrarian and null case
